## Integration acceptance and feasibility

Test each rule in isolation, then combinations: weekend + external whitelist + theory block; fairness + section gaps; adjunct category + incoming availability + SUST break preference; ECL/Physics reservations + three-meeting sessionals + same-room theory blocks; six/seven slot variants and Friday blocks. Include joint sections, co-teachers, no faculty, no room candidates, contradictory declarations and an intentionally insufficient solve budget.

Use a validator independent of placement to recompute counts, intervals, resource availability, patterns and policy-specific day/gap metrics. Full validation must apply equally to engine output, uploaded schedules, manual draft edits and publication. Never infer completeness from the exported grid: service rows are separate and missing source requirements may never reach that grid. A failed preference is reported; a failed mandatory rule prevents publication. The first browser phase does not depend on implementing all these constraints in advance.

## Additional capability CD1 and conflict/outcome contract

CD1 is additional to the original 16 requirements. The Course Distribution page is **unsupported**: upload/report and course eligibility are not that page. Source field editability, formulas/cached values, derived fields, affected symbols and the proposed immutable-original/accepted-draft pipeline are specified in [record 18](18-committee-decisions-and-policy-baseline-2026-11-04.md). Its accepted corrections must feed the exact normalized input used by all downstream consumers, with original values and edit provenance retained.

D6 applies to every row: never silently weaken a mandatory rule or auto-enable a permission. Report affected requirement/resource/slot and an actionable configuration correction; accept an authorized traceable revision and revalidate/regenerate. Separate proven infeasibility, time exhaustion, unknown outcome, invalid/incomplete input, room-allocation failure and network/service failure. UNKNOWN is not necessarily timeout; a feasible time-limited incumbent still requires full validation. Existing _infeasible_message wording is not compliant with this future contract.

Additional acceptance: independent weekend pairs with max-min off-day difference one; permanent attendance days with no classes; adjunct same course in multiple sections; low-credit theory including 1.5 credits; sessional same-lab break pair disabled/enabled without affecting other rules; formula-derived source corrections; complete accepted-draft round trip and stale-result denial. Unresolved three-credit count/spread and adjunct bands stay unresolved until dependent implementation.
